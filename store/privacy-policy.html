Privacy policy

Draft dated 13 November 2026.

The publisher must complete the data-controller name and support/privacy contact, review the configured content host and publish this policy at a public HTTPS URL before submission. This draft does not identify an invented business or contact.

What the app stores

The app works without an account. Favorites, visited-region markers, history, notes, objectives, local profile names, collections and preferences are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not receive your personal notes or plans.

Network requests

The bundled edition works offline. If a publisher feed is configured, the app may periodically request verified editorial content over HTTPS. The content host can see normal connection metadata such as your IP address and request time. The app does not attach your profile, history, notes, favorites or device advertising identifier. The publisher must document the host, any operational logs, processors, legal basis and retention before enabling that service. In the default build no feed or push notification service is configured.

External links and files

Source links open in your browser and the third-party site’s policies apply. You choose files for JSON backup import/export through Android’s system picker. Exported files are not encrypted by this app. Anyone with access to an export can read its contents. Files may be stored with a provider you select under that provider’s terms.

Permissions

The app declares internet and network-state access. It does not request location, contacts, broad storage, microphone, camera or notification permissions. No game-account credentials are requested or stored.

Control and retention

Data remains until you remove it, erase a local profile, clear app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Exports remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately. Export is available in the app. No server-side user profile exists. Contact the identified publisher for privacy questions about any configured feed host.

Audience and independence

This companion concerns a mature game and is not designed for children. It is independent and unofficial and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Changes

The publisher must update this policy if data practices change. Current bundled information is visible in the app’s About and privacy screen.
